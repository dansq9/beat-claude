### Part 2: Risk tiering system

Each automated output carries six yes/no fields: `legal`, `confidential`, `top_account`, `external`, `revenue`, `irreversible`. First match wins:

```
legal?                                  -> RED
confidential AND external?              -> RED
top_account AND revenue?                -> RED
external AND irreversible?              -> YELLOW (operator approves each send)
external OR revenue OR top_account?     -> YELLOW
else                                    -> GREEN (auto-execute / digest)
```

## What breaks it

- **Hand-tagged risk fields:** if the automation can't emit `legal`/`confidential` reliably, red items slip to green. Detect this with the weekly 10% green sample and any external complaint. Fallback: keyword tripwires (contract, liability, client name + numbers) force yellow.
- **Owner overload:** routing reds to account leads fails if one lead owns most top accounts. Watch per-owner open reds.
- **SLA theater:** people may mark items done to hit the SLA (Q-13 already shows this). Done requires an outcome record.

## What stays human

Contract and liability positions (Q-11). Any message to a top-account executive (Q-15). Anything containing client data (Q-16). Outreach to people who work at our clients (Q-18). Killing a workflow. The automation can tag and route all of these, but it doesn't decide them.
